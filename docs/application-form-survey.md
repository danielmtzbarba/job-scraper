No applicant data was entered, files uploaded, or applications submitted.

## Reusable question catalogue

The eight finest-jobs forms share required name, postal code, city, country, email, professional-experience category, and CV upload fields. Some also require street, phone, or salutation. They expose optional birth date, language levels, vocational training, highest degree, degree title, motivation letter, and other supporting documents. The forms have employer-specific data-processing consent and an optional finest-jobs talent-pool choice; those choices must be shown explicitly during review, not inferred from stored candidate facts.

The other two paths show why the workflow needs site inspection: mgm has a location feasibility check and group-sharing choice, while BWI separates personal details, documents, and questions into stages. The survey only establishes what was visible without entering applicant data. A later pilot should discover any conditional or subsequent fields before treating an application as ready to submit.

For a progressive application facts sheet, candidate facts include contact/address, work authorization and nationality, professional-experience category, education, language levels, location and on-site preferences, weekly hours, notice period, and earliest availability. Salary and compensation expectations may need a job-specific answer. Every stored fact should retain its source, the user's approval, and when it was approved. The prepared PDFs remain separate versioned artifacts selected per application. Consent and optional talent-pool decisions are application-specific choices rather than reusable facts.
