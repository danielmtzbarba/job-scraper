# Job Matching Language

These terms distinguish role selection from candidate fit assessment.

## Language

**Role family**:
One of the five kinds of engineering work represented by a reviewed career profile. A posting can belong to a role family even when the candidate lacks some required skills.

**Profile classification**:
The decision that assigns a posting to its best role family or identifies it as outside all target role families. It does not judge the candidate's fit.

**Out of scope**:
A posting whose central responsibilities belong to none of the five target role families. It remains tracked but receives no fit score.
_Avoid_: Low fit

**Low fit**:
A scored posting within a target role family whose requirements align poorly with the selected career profile.
_Avoid_: Out of scope

**Fit needs review**:
A posting within a target role family whose description lacks essential information for a reliable fit score. This is separate from a duplicate-posting review.
_Avoid_: Duplicate review
